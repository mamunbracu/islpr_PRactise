For many families, the start of the school year arrives at the worst possible time. Straight after the expense of Christmas and New Year, parents must pay for new uniforms, shoes, hats, books and stationery.

Courtney Jarvis is one parent feeling the pressure. She says the cost of basic items such as school shirts, shorts and hats is difficult to manage so soon after the holidays. Her situation is harder because her son Silvester, who is starting preschool, has muscular dystrophy, a condition that brings significant extra costs.

In North Queensland, help is available through School Savvy, a program run by Centacare NQ. It allows parents to buy uniforms and school supplies at low prices. Last year the program assisted 2,500 families, and this year it is expanding to the Burdekin, Mount Isa and Cloncurry regions.

Centacare says demand for discounted school supplies has grown this year because the high cost of living is putting real pressure on households. Parent Maria Tracey is one of those who benefited. The program saved her a couple of hundred dollars, which she says makes a big difference when everyday prices keep rising.

Another response has come from the community itself. In 2020, Alison Schutt and her husband Jan started a charity called Stationery Aid. The idea came from their own home: their children kept bringing back exercise books and stationery that had hardly been used, yet no organisation would accept them as donations.

Stationery Aid now collects used exercise books and stationery and gives them a second life. Volunteers remove the used pages and students' names, then repair or cover the books so they look new before passing them on to students in need.

The couple considered sending the items overseas, but decided to focus on Australian students because the need in their own community is so great. Stationery Aid estimates that about one per cent of Queensland students need some help to start the school year with the basic equipment they require.

Local sewing groups also contribute, making fabric pencil cases and library bags so that students receive a complete set of supplies.
